LAYUNA

Sleep in the Emirates is a negotiation between heat and cool air, between long days and short nights, between travel and return. We begin from a single principle: a surface measured, composed and tuned to the one person who rests on it – posture, weight, climate and habit.

Air-conditioned rooms and warm nights ask different things of a bed. Breathable covers in cotton, wool and linen, and natural latex that lets heat escape, keep the surface even and quiet, whether the room is cooled to a whisper or the evening has not yet released the day.

The work begins with a conversation and ends with a fitting. We listen to the way you lie, the way you turn, the way you wake. Firmness is adjusted, profiles refined and details revisited until the result feels inevitable – quiet, supportive and entirely your own.

Layers of foam are chosen zone by zone – shoulders, waist, hips and legs – so that the spine rests in its natural line and the body is carried rather than pressed. Each layer has its own density, its own height and its own purpose; together they behave as one calm, continuous surface.

Natural materials age with grace. They breathe, they recover, they settle into the habits of the sleeper. Nothing is glued where it can be stitched; nothing is hurried where it can be done by hand. Craftsmanship is not an ornament here – it is the structure.

A place of rest composed for one person, in one home, under the desert sky. Conceived in Dubai, made to your measure, and refined for as long as it takes. A perfect night begins long before you lie down: with listening, with precision, with time.
