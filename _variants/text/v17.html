Sleep in the Emirates is a negotiation between heat and cool air, between long days and short nights, between travel and return. We begin from a single principle: a surface measured, composed and tuned to the one person who rests on it – posture, weight, climate and habit.1 Layers of foam are chosen zone by zone2, with breathable covers3 and natural latex that lets heat escape. The work ends with a fitting4 — quiet, supportive and entirely your own5.

  1. 1Posture, weight, climate and habit are considered before anything is made.
  2. 2Shoulders, waist, hips and legs; each region is given its own density and height.
  3. 3Cotton, wool and linen; materials that breathe, recover and age with grace.
  4. 4Held at home; firmness is adjusted and details revisited until it feels inevitable.
  5. 5One person, one home, one measure. Nothing is chosen from a shelf.
  6. 6Conceived in Dubai, finished by hand, refined for as long as it takes.
