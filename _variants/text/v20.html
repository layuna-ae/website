Dear sleeper,

I wanted to tell you how a night should be made. It begins not with a catalogue but with you – the way you lie, the way you turn, the weight you bring home from a long day in the heat.

Layers of foam, chosen zone by zone. Cotton, wool and linen that breathe. Nothing glued where it can be stitched.

Then we sit, we listen, and we adjust, until it feels inevitable.

with care, from Dubai

Every sleep surface begins with a body, not a catalogue. Sleep in the Emirates is a negotiation between heat and cool air, between long days and short nights, between travel and return. We begin from a single principle: a surface measured, composed and tuned to the one person who rests on it – posture, weight, climate and habit.
