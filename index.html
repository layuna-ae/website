Sleep in the Emirates moves between heat and cool air, between long days and short nights. We begin from one principle: a surface measured, composed and tuned to the single person who rests on it – posture, weight, climate and habit.

Layers of foam are chosen zone by zone – shoulders, waist, hips and legs – so the spine rests in its natural line and the body is carried rather than pressed. Natural materials breathe, recover and settle into the habits of the sleeper.

Each surface begins with a conversation and ends with a fitting. Firmness is adjusted and details revisited until it feels inevitable – quiet, supportive, entirely your own. Conceived in Dubai, made to your measure.

يبدأ كل سطح بحديثٍ وينتهي بتجربةِ ضبط. تُعدَّل درجة الصلابة وتُراجَع التفاصيل حتى يبدو الأمر حتميًّا – هادئًا وداعمًا وملكًا لك وحدك. صُمّم في دبي، ويُصنع على قياسك.
